# Code of Conduct

oneClient is a community of people building and using a developer tool. We want it to be a place where anyone can ask a question,
report a bug or send a patch without being treated badly for it.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to the project or to the tool they're asking about.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good faith.
- Keep discussions on topic and issues actionable: what happened, what you expected, how to reproduce it.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, or spamming issues, discussions and pull requests.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find hostile in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these standards, and may restrict participation for
repeat or serious violations. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, or open a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/AhmedKhalil777/OneClient/security/advisories/new) addressed to the maintainers. Reports are
handled privately.

This text follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version
2.1, which applies to the project as well.
